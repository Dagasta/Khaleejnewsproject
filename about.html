Built for the Modern Newsroom.

Atheer bridges the gap between global intelligence and Arabic media publishing.

The News Cycle Doesn't Wait.

In the high-stakes environment of modern journalism, speed and accuracy are everything. Editorial teams across the GCC face a dual challenge: monitoring thousands of global sources for breaking news, and accurately translating that complex, nuanced information into professional Arabic journalism before the competition does.

Traditionally, this required multiple disconnected tools—an RSS reader, Google Translate, a text editor, and a CMS. We built Atheer to collapse this entire workflow into a single, unified operating system.

Our Mission

To empower journalists, translators, and media agencies with a unified workspace that seamlessly combines real-time global intelligence gathering with elite AI-assisted Arabic translation.

Who is Atheer For?

News Agencies & Editors: Stay ahead of global trends with our Live Pulse Radar, curating the most important stories tailored for the UAE and broader GCC markets.

Translators & Copywriters: Stop wrestling with literal, robotic translations. Atheer's AI Studio is fine-tuned for journalistic Arabic, offering formats ranging from full articles to brief summaries and headlines.

Corporate Intelligence Teams: For businesses that need to monitor global markets (Crypto, Gold, Finance) and distill that information into internal Arabic briefs instantly.
